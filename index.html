Our Story

FreshMart was founded in 2020 with a simple mission: to make fresh, quality groceries accessible to everyone. What started as a small neighborhood store has grown into a trusted online platform serving over 50,000 happy customers.

We partner directly with local farmers and trusted suppliers to ensure every product you receive is of the highest quality. Our commitment to freshness, affordability, and convenience has made us a household name.

Today, we deliver to thousands of homes every day — because we believe fresh food should never be a luxury.

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does delivery take?

We deliver within 30 minutes in most areas. Express delivery available for selected locations.

What is the minimum order amount?

No minimum order. Free delivery on orders over $49.

How do I return a product?

Request a return within 7 days of delivery from your Orders page. Free pickup.

What payment methods do you accept?

Visa, Mastercard, Bkash, Nagad, and Cash on Delivery.

How do I track my order?

Use the Track Order page with your order number, or go to My Orders and click Track.
